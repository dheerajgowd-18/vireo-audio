Data Audit & Operational Allocation Plan for Q3 Support Training Budget (₹4,00,000)

An audit across all 11,750 support tickets reveals that allocating the ₹4,00,000 training budget to the raw company-wide "Bottom 10" list would misdirect capital. Six of those ten are Tier 2 specialists handling complex escalations, and four are frontline staff on the hardware triage rota. Furthermore, the ₹21.22L in Pulse 2 replacement costs is concentrated in lot PL2-2510-3—a supplier quality issue that support training cannot solve. Highest-return impact requires focusing strictly on frontline Tier 1 Chat and Email workflow coaching.

Primary Target Business Outcome
"Reduce Tier 1 frontline (Chat and Email) above-median handle time from 6.54h to 4.86h (weighted peer medians: Chat 5.61h → 3.81h, Email 8.49h → 7.07h), eliminating 624.9 excess agent-hours per quarter, representing approximately ₹1,03,102 per quarter in modeled recoverable staffing capacity."
Under Policy §4 (₹165/hr fully loaded labor), this recovers ₹4.12 Lakhs in annualized labor capacity across four quarters, effectively amortizing the ₹4.0L budget while safeguarding CSAT above our 3.33 historical baseline.

Core Analytical Findings

  • The "Bottom 10" Ranking Suffers from Structural Tier Conflation: Sorting agents by raw CSAT places all six Tier 2 Escalation specialists (A3039–A3044, CSAT 2.42–2.83) and four hardware triage agents (A3004–A3007, CSAT 2.98–3.04) at the bottom. Under Policy §6, Tier 2 cases are multi-touch investigations measured across days, not weeks. Comparing them against frontline staff answering routine delivery questions unfairly penalizes them for handling distressed customers.
  • Pulse 2 Replacements Stem from Hardware Batch Quality, Not Support Execution: Warranty replacements consumed ₹34,15,990 across 1,896 units under Policy §5 (refuting Finance's flat ₹2,500 estimate of ₹47.40L by a ₹13.24L overstatement). The Pulse 2 model accounts for 61.50% of all replacements (1,166 units; ₹21,22,120). Production lot PL2-2510-3 exhibits a severe 40.94% replacement rate (70 units replaced across 171 tickets). Text analysis identifies charging pin contact failure signatures. Agent training cannot resolve manufacturing defects.
  • Frontline Handle Time is Inflated by Ticket Aging and Shorthand Notes: While 93–95% of frontline tickets resolve in under an hour, 5–7% linger for days due to incomplete discovery. Crucially, 908 tickets (7.73%) contain uninformative shorthand notes (words ≤ 2 OR characters ≤ 9, e.g., "-", "done", "cx ok"), forcing successive agents into redundant diagnostic loops.

Operating Risks & Caveats

  • Capacity vs. Cash: ₹1.03L/quarter represents recoverable labor capacity to absorb growth without hiring, not direct payroll reductions.
  • CSAT Protection: Coaching must focus on eliminating stagnant ticket aging rather than rushing active customer conversations.
  • Hardware Containment: The ₹21.22L Pulse 2 warranty exposure requires supplier remediation and must not be treated as a support metric.

  • Quality Escalation: Deliver lot PL2-2510-3 findings to Hardware Engineering and Supply Chain for supplier warranty recovery.
